## Objective

Build the pure bounded reduction orchestrator and the typed CLI and HTTP neighbor providers. A reduction may accept a smaller stimulus only when a new eligible typed evaluation is comparable to the full baseline map and reproduces the exact canonical sorted `candidate_execution_key -> projection_fingerprint` map. Evaluation construction enforces same-domain content-digest nonoverlap with the baseline and earlier evaluations; the reducer cannot prove physical execution or freshness. Reference process edges must create the new attempts, worlds, processes, and captures they report. Comparability requires the same plan, envelope, stimulus-independent comparison basis, expected roster, eligible set, exclusions, and exclusion classifications; the per-run admission artifact and purpose may differ. Partition shape, member sets, outcome count, support count, cluster ordinal, a naked digest, or a display DTO are never preservation authorities.

The unit must produce replayable provenance and only these grades:

- `UNCHANGED`: no smaller preserving stimulus was accepted; limitations remain explicit and this is not a minimality claim;
- `BEST_KNOWN`: at least one preserving reduction exists, but the strong durable authority was not supplied, including when a budget ended, the final sweep is incomplete, a final direct neighbor is unresolved, or the logical sweep completed without a supplied authority; and
- `ONE_MINIMAL_UNDER(reducer_set_digest)`: the exact compiled-`WorldPlan` shrink budget governed a limitation-free complete logical sweep, every enumerated valid direct neighbor has a typed nonreused `CHANGES` evaluation, and the exact draft was durably published and reopened. This grade does not itself prove physical freshness.

Do not claim a smallest behavior, global minimum, root cause, semantic equivalence, or correctness.

## Locked truth boundaries

`internal/compare` remains the authority for `CandidateOutcomeMap`, its typed `PreservationMapDigest`, and the opaque comparability-first result. The reducer consumes full typed maps or that opaque result; it must not compare a naked digest, reconstruct maps from display clusters, require identical per-run admission artifacts across different stimuli, or substitute `OutcomeArtifactDigest`. CLI and HTTP keep their own typed stimuli, adapter-level reduction measures, explicit fixed-versus-reducible scopes, and deterministic neighbor functions. `internal/reduce` owns only search order, budgets, tri-valued orchestration, transcript construction, and a pure logical final-sweep draft. It must not import Git, process, HTTP, CLI, store, or server implementations. A narrow `internal/store` edge imports that inward draft and issues an opaque capability only after atomic publication and reopen. A thin outward `internal/reduction` composition service imports both packages and is the sole constructor of the authoritative grade. This package split is required: a reducer-owned store interface would be forgeable, while concrete bidirectional imports would form a Go cycle.

Do not change the sealed U3/U4 stimulus identities merely to add reduction ordering. U5 measures are separate canonical tuples whose definition digest is bound into the reducer-set digest. That digest also binds the exact finite rule set and policy scope, including fixed execution shape, enabled transform classes, and any pinned semantic fixture/seed anchors. The run's stimulus identities bind current reducible member values. Local minimality quantifies only over the final stimulus's direct neighborhood under that policy.

An evaluation is exactly `PRESERVES`, `CHANGES`, or `UNRESOLVED`. First assess full-map comparability. Incomparable maps are `UNRESOLVED`; comparable maps with equal exact labeled-map digests are `PRESERVES`; comparable maps with different exact labeled-map digests are `CHANGES`. Every unstable, incomplete, cancelled, timed-out, stale, or budget-precluded result is also `UNRESOLVED`. Never encode this as a Boolean.

No execution evidence may be cached or reused. Pure parsing and canonical transforms of immutable bytes may be memoized, but each evaluated neighbor needs new attempt artifacts, worlds, processes, and captures supplied by the evaluator. The reducer records evidence links and never asserts freshness from a nonce alone.
