## Locked boundary

U2 owns two impure edges and no observation semantics:

1. an immutable, filter-free Git-object reader that recursively parses independently rehashed raw tree objects, admits only independently rehashed regular/executable blobs, persists a canonical manifest, and exclusively publishes a complete fresh tree; and
2. a Darwin one-shot process owner that allocates new roots, spawns one byte-measured absolute tool without a shell or ambient environment, captures bounded streams, and cooperatively tears down its measured process group.

The design authority is `docs/decisions/0002-u2-impure-boundary.md`, amended by `research/deep-dive/09-u2-design-red-team.md`. This is trusted-local execution with the operator's full filesystem and host-network permissions. It is neither a sandbox nor a confidentiality boundary. Canonical-path revalidation narrows ordinary mutation windows but does not resist a concurrent same-user filesystem attacker.

U2 must not construct `CapturedObservation`, `StableBatch`, divergence, reduction, eligibility, or ruling authority. Process and materialization results remain receipts supplied by the physical edge to later adapters.
