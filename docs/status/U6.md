## Boundary actually implemented

U6a turns the U5 reduced divergent witness into a bounded durable decision context. It adds:

1. a private create-once content-addressed semantic object store;
2. a fixed, full-token compare-and-swap study head from `SOURCE_PLAN` through `RULING`;
3. a live physical confirmation run and strict inert `FreshConfirmation` restart record;
4. strict `Choicepoint` and `DecisionRecord` runtime codecs with checked-in schema/example field parity;
5. a candidate-neutral blind semantic DTO and five-state decision session;
6. sealed compile eligibility rather than a caller-coercible Boolean; and
7. predecessor/action-bound opaque publication capabilities for confirmation, Choicepoint, and ruling promotion.

This boundary stops at `RULING`. It does not emit or execute a standalone contract. P07/U6b owns that next transition.

## Red-team corrections carried into implementation

U6 did not rubber-stamp the original P06 prose. Independent architecture, publication-authority, documentation, checker, and hostile-mutation reviews locked these corrections:

1. A `Choicepoint` is the immutable fresh-confirmed decision context; the distinct `DecisionRecord` is the local-caller-attributed ruling. Neither artifact proves a human identity or authenticates authorship.
2. Pure session finalization accepts all five semantic actions. Durable promotion accepts `ALLOW_OBSERVED`, `CUSTOM_EXPECTATION`, `REJECT_ALL`, and `DEFER`; `REFINE` returns `REFINE_REQUIRES_SUCCESSOR_STUDY` before object publication or head mutation.
3. Only `ALLOW_OBSERVED` and separately reviewed `CUSTOM_EXPECTATION` can yield the sealed `CompilableRuling` variant. `REJECT_ALL`, `DEFER`, and `REFINE` yield a sealed noncompilable variant. A wire Boolean is never construction authority.
4. The head transition API is typed. Raw `advanceHead` is unexported and restricted to the six reviewed stage methods through `RULING`; callers must present the complete prior `HeadToken`—store instance, study, revision, stage, current kind/digest, previous object, lineage root, and head digest—not merely a digest or revision.
5. A losing or stale CAS attempt may not publish a successor semantic object. Publication happens only after the locked current head is reopened and exactly compared, then the new head and object are reopened before success is returned.
6. Semantic objects are create-once by exact digest and bytes. Publication uses a private same-directory temporary, sync, hard-link create-if-absent, cleanup, reopen, and exact rehash. An existing destination is never overwritten, and reopening repeats filesystem-shape, digest, canonical-byte, kind, and owner-codec validation.
7. Publication validators are binding-capability validators, not complete schema validators. Full records are first constructed or parsed by their owning service; the opaque capability then binds exact canonical bytes, digest, predecessor, and—for rulings—action.
8. Fresh confirmation is relative, typed evidence rather than a timestamp claim. A fresh 32-byte challenge is domain-digested, each exact schedule ordinal receives a derived nonce, every result must be a finalized `CONFIRMATION` world, and physical evidence identities must be disjoint from both the current run and discovery/reduction ledgers.
9. Confirmation compares complete labeled outcome maps only after revalidating the exact candidate roster and eligibility/exclusion classifications. A freshly executed excluded candidate may remain `UNSTABLE` if its key and exclusion classification are unchanged; a changed eligible/excluded disposition refuses readiness.
10. Confirmation retry is a new run with a new challenge and schedule. A crash may leave already-finalized immutable objects or earlier nonadvancing evidence, but U6 writes no crash-time `PARTIAL` status object and cannot resume the interrupted run.
11. Strict restart parsers reject noncanonical JSON, duplicate members, wrong kinds, unknown/omitted members, changed closed facts, resealed physical-fact tampering, candidate/ordinal swaps, and mismatched predecessor or action bindings.
12. Blind aliases bind the exact Choicepoint digest and projection fingerprint, never the first supporting candidate. Card order is fingerprint-only; input candidate order and support count cannot affect it. One alias expands to every supporting confirmed outcome.
13. The blind DTO's closed semantic shape omits system-supplied candidate identity, producer metadata, support counts, total candidate count, input order, and reveal data. Caller-authored scenario or behavior content may still reveal identity; DOM, accessibility, CSS, and visual anonymity remain U8 work.
14. The decision session requires exact review-surface visitation, explicit selected fields, provisional action before reveal, and a rationale for any post-reveal change. Its presentation markers prove only that surfaces were recorded as visited, not comprehension or debiasing.
15. Decision receipts are inert verbatim references with opaque association. U6 does not interpret didrun grades as product conformance and cannot reconstruct didrun authority from serialized receipt text.
16. The architecture checker owns a closed U6 production-file manifest and exact internal import lattice, rejects symlinks/nonregular files/invalid UTF-8/package drift/dot imports, freezes authority issuers and seal construction, and closes raw-head, compile-eligibility, alias, and blind-comparator extension paths.
17. The checker self-test freezes a checksum-pinned roster of 49 forced-failure cases, including compile-valid wrapper, method-value, intermediate-interface, comment-camouflage, Unicode, schema-drift, duplicate-JSON, ownership, and filesystem attacks.
18. The integrated mutation driver uses 17 distinct source mutations with admitted absolute Go/clang executables, exact private seeds, named-test enforcement, compilation-failure classification, and fresh A/B/A closure. A missing executable declaration fails closed rather than silently trusting ambient `PATH`.

## Store format and lineage

The Darwin reference layout is:

```text
<root>/objects/sha256/<first-two-hex>/<full-hex-digest>
<root>/studies/<study-digest-hex>/head.json
<root>/studies/<study-digest-hex>/.head.lock
<root>/private-captures/
```

Semantic payloads carry the closed `countershape/v1` schema version and domain-separated SHA-256 identity. The store itself has no migration protocol or promise that this on-disk layout is a stable public format.

The implemented advancing lineage is exact:

```text
SOURCE_PLAN -> BASELINE -> DIVERGENCE -> REDUCTION
            -> CONFIRMATION -> CHOICEPOINT_READY -> RULING
```

`RESIDUE` is a declared later stage but has no U6a transition. `PARTIAL` and `CANCELLED` are not resumable U6 status workflows.

## Bounded resource profile

- Canonical JSON input and any stored semantic object: at most 1 MiB.
- Study label, decision actor: at most 512 UTF-8 bytes each.
- Choicepoint scenario: at most 8 KiB; nested raw evidence: at most 600 KiB.
- Per-candidate reveal display/provenance text: at most 2 KiB combined.
- Decision annotation/rationale payload: at most 32 KiB.
- Confirmation retained projection-proof bytes: at most 512 KiB.
- Confirmation challenge: exactly 32 random bytes before domain hashing.
- Trial count: exact candidate-roster cardinality multiplied by declared repetitions and charged through the typed observation budget.

These are parser and orchestration ceilings, not proof of resistance to hostile same-user resource exhaustion.
